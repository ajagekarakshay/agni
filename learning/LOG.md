# Agni learning log

This log records evidence that cannot be inferred reliably from code or commits.
Add one row after each session. Leave missed days unrecorded; they are not failed
sessions and do not create a backlog.

## Session history

| Date | Roadmap item | Commit or branch | Minutes | Recall (0–3) | Error tags | Next reviews | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |

Recall scale:

- **0:** could not reconstruct the idea without substantial help;
- **1:** recognized it but could not derive or implement it independently;
- **2:** mostly independent with a small conceptual or implementation gap;
- **3:** independently derived, explained, implemented, and defended tradeoffs.

Suggested error tags: `math`, `numerical`, `lifetime`, `api-contract`, `testing`,
`complexity`, `allocation`, `measurement`, `tooling`, and `explanation`.

Review dates should normally include approximately 1, 3, 7, and 21 days after a
topic is first learned. Later reviews may be removed when retrieval is reliably
independent.
